Privacy, in short

  • Your journal stays on your iPhone. Moments, photos, notes, amounts and pockets are saved only on your device.
  • We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

This policy explains how the Pockets app for iPhone (“Pockets”, “we”) handles your information. The short answer: we don’t collect it.

What you put in the app

Everything you add to Pockets (your name, birthday, income, moments, notes, spending amounts, pockets, theme choices and photos) is stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We never receive, see or store it.

Photos

Pockets only uses photos you choose to add to a moment. It does not scan or upload your photo library. A copy of each photo you add is kept inside the app so it shows in your journal.

Backups and exports

When you tap Export data or Back up to iCloud Drive, Pockets creates a backup file and opens Apple’s share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). Files saved to iCloud Drive are handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

Subscriptions

Purchases, free trials, renewals and offer codes are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Pockets contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not share data with anyone. Apple may give developers anonymous, combined App Store statistics (such as download counts) if you have allowed this in your iPhone settings.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from Settings › Data Storage › Reset app, or by deleting the app. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you exported are kept wherever you saved them until you remove them.

Children

Pockets is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support

How do I back up my journal?

Go to Settings › Data Storage › Back up to iCloud Drive, tap Save to Files, then choose iCloud Drive. Doing this regularly keeps a safe copy if your phone is lost or replaced.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, back up to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install Pockets, then go to Settings › Data Storage › Import data and choose your backup file.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Your journal stays on your iPhone. You can still open it, look through every moment and export it. Adding new moments needs an active plan.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Settings › Your Plan › Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I use an offer code?

Open Settings › Your Plan › Redeem a code, or tap “Have a code?” on the subscription screen, and enter your code.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Pockets, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.
